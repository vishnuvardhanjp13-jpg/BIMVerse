09 / Product information

Before you start.

What format do I receive?

The intended BEP deliverable is an editable Microsoft Word (.docx) document with a structured Table of Contents.

Can I customize the BEP?

Yes. Replace project-specific placeholders and adapt requirements, responsibilities, naming conventions, workflows and deliverables to your project.

What is the difference between the BEP versions?

Tier 3 provides the standard framework. Tier 2 adds a completed reference example. Tier 1 adds professional review and consultation support.

What is the ISO 19650 Appendix Package?

It is a complementary BIM information-management package intended to extend the documentation and supporting information surrounding an ISO 19650-oriented workflow.

Is this an official Autodesk or ISO document?

No. This is an independent BIM template and information management product. It is not an Autodesk product and should be tailored to the applicable project, contractual and organizational requirements.
